`src/03_text_font.ts` includes outline subsets derived from DejaVu Sans,
DejaVu Serif and DejaVu Sans Mono. The complete font copyright, trademark,
permission and disclaimer notice is preserved in
`src/assets/DejaVuSans-LICENSE.txt`. The same notice is included in the About
window of each standalone HTML build and in the embedded source ZIP.
